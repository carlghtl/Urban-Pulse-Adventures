Safety Policy — Urban Pulse Adventures

Urban Pulse Adventures is committed to the safety and well‑being of every guest, guide and community member. This Safety Policy describes responsibilities, procedures and standards we apply to reduce risk while delivering authentic urban experiences across Nairobi and surrounding areas.

Scope and Applicability

This policy applies to all activities and services provided by Urban Pulse Adventures, including guided tours, transfers, community visits and third‑party supplier services operated on our behalf.

Customer Responsibilities

  • Provide accurate health, mobility and emergency contact information at booking.
  • Disclose any medical conditions, allergies or medication requirements prior to participation.
  • Follow the guide’s instructions and local laws; failure to do so may result in removal from the tour without refund.
  • Arrive at the meeting point on time and prepared with appropriate clothing, water and identification.
  • Keep personal belongings secure and act responsibly in public and community settings.

Guide & Staff Responsibilities

  • All guides complete training in first aid, risk assessment, cultural sensitivity and emergency procedures.
  • Guides conduct a pre‑tour briefing that covers itinerary, expected hazards and community etiquette.
  • Guides carry first‑aid kits, emergency contacts and basic communications equipment.
  • Staff escalate incidents immediately to Urban Pulse operations and to emergency services where required.

Emergency Procedures

In an emergency the guide will:

  1. Assess the situation and secure the group;
  2. Provide first aid within their competence;
  3. Contact local emergency services and Urban Pulse operations;
  4. Assist with transport to the nearest medical facility if required; and
  5. Notify the guest’s emergency contact as authorised in the booking form.

Urban Pulse maintains written emergency escalation plans and a register of nearest medical facilities for every route.

Medical Disclosures & Fitness to Participate

Guests must declare any condition that might affect their ability to participate. Urban Pulse may require a physician’s clearance for guests with significant medical conditions. Participation is at the customer’s own risk where the customer elects not to disclose relevant health information.

Risk Acknowledgement

Certain urban experiences involve exposure to traffic, uneven surfaces, crowded public areas and variable weather. By taking part in our activities guests acknowledge these inherent risks and agree to follow safety directions issued by guides.

Transportation Safety

  • Vehicles used for transfers are licensed and drivers are vetted and instructed in safe driving practices.
  • Seat‑belts must be worn where provided; child seats are available on request for transfers.
  • Drivers monitor real‑time flight arrivals for airport transfers; waiting and parking charges may apply.

Community Visit Guidelines

  • Visits are coordinated with community leaders and host organisations.
  • Guests must request permission before photographing or recording individuals.
  • Gifts or donations to community members must be discussed with guides to avoid unintended harm.

Child Safety

Minors must be supervised by a parent or guardian at all times. Guides will not accept legal responsibility for unaccompanied minors. For children under 12, special risk assessments may be required for certain activities.

Personal Belongings

Urban Pulse accepts no liability for loss or damage to personal belongings. Guests should keep valuables secure and insure items such as cameras and laptops. Report any theft or loss to the guide and local police immediately.

Weather & Force Majeure

We monitor weather and local safety advisories. Urban Pulse may modify, reschedule or cancel services where conditions pose a safety risk. Where cancellations are necessary we will offer alternatives or refunds as set out in our Terms & Conditions.

Compliance with Kenyan Law & Local Authorities

Urban Pulse operates in accordance with Kenyan laws and regulations, including guidance under the Tourism Act and directives from local authorities. Guests must comply with instructions from official authorities during tours.

Reporting Incidents

Report any safety incidents or concerns to your guide immediately and follow up with Urban Pulse via urbanpulseadventures254.org@gmail.com within 48 hours. We investigate all reported incidents and keep records in compliance with applicable regulations.

Policy Review

This policy is reviewed annually or after any significant incident. Updates will be posted on this page with the revision date.
