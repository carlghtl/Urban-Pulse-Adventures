Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Last updated: April 2026

1. Introduction

Urban Pulse Adventures ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting the privacy and security of personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use and store personal information in compliance with the Data Protection Act, 2019 (Kenya) and applicable international standards.

2. Controller & Contact

The data controller is Urban Pulse Adventures. For privacy enquiries or to exercise your rights please contact our Data Protection Officer at: urbanpulseadventures254.org@gmail.com or urbanpulseadventures254.org@gmail.com.

3. Personal Data We Collect

a. Data You Provide Directly

  • Identity and contact details: full name, email, telephone, postal address
  • Booking information: selected tours, dates, number of guests, preferences
  • Payment and billing information (payment card data processed by secure third‑party providers)
  • Health and accessibility data provided to enable safe participation
  • Communications: feedback, enquiries and support correspondence

b. Automatically Collected Data

  • Device, browser and operating system information
  • IP address and approximate location
  • Usage data (pages visited, clicks, session times) and analytics data
  • Cookies and similar technologies (details in our Cookie Policy)

4. Purposes and Legal Bases for Processing

We process personal data for the following purposes and lawful bases:

  • Performance of a contract: to confirm bookings and provide services
  • Legal compliance: to meet tax, health and safety or regulatory obligations
  • Legitimate interests: to operate the business, prevent fraud and improve services
  • Consent: for marketing communications where explicit consent is obtained

5. Sharing Personal Data

We share data with:

  • Service providers who facilitate bookings (guides, transport, accommodation)
  • Payment processors for secure payment handling
  • Regulatory or law enforcement authorities where required by law
  • Third parties involved in dispute resolution or legal claims

We do not sell personal data to third parties.

6. International Transfers

Personal data may be transferred to countries outside Kenya for processing. Where transfers occur, we use appropriate safeguards such as contractual clauses or rely on recognised adequacy mechanisms.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for service delivery, legal compliance and dispute resolution. Typical retention periods include:

  • Financial and booking records: 7 years (statutory requirement)
  • Support enquiries: 2–5 years depending on the nature of the record
  • Marketing consents: until consent is withdrawn

8. Security Measures

We use industry‑standard technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including encryption in transit (TLS), access controls, and regular security assessments. No system is impenetrable — report any suspected breach to urbanpulseadventures254.org@gmail.com immediately.

9. Your Rights

You have rights under the Data Protection Act, including:

  • Access to your personal data
  • Correction of inaccurate data
  • Erasure in certain circumstances
  • Objecting to processing on legitimate interest grounds
  • Restriction of processing and data portability

To exercise these rights contact urbanpulseadventures254.org@gmail.com. We will respond within statutory timeframes.

10. Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies to operate the website and to collect analytics data. See our Cookie Policy for details and how to manage cookie preferences.

11. Children

Our services are aimed at adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 18. If you believe a child has provided personal data please contact us to request deletion.

12. Complaints

If you wish to make a complaint about our data practices, contact urbanpulseadventures254.org@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner in Kenya.

13. Changes to this Policy

We will update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be published on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
